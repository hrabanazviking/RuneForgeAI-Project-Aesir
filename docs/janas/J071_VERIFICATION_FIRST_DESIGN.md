## 1. Verdict — and the design + rule, stated before building

**J-071 closes as a design + accounting slice, complete on its own terms:**

- The design and the verdict rule below were **pre-registered** (written into the new module's header and this section before any test ran), in the road's standing discipline.
- The mechanical half is built and proven: `core/speculative_verify.mojo` — one greedy verify round **derived, never asserted**, from a proposer's draft list and the target model's own greedy list; emission built from the target list only; acceptance a strict prefix; KV/context rollback exact; the greedy round's zero-RNG-draw invariant enforced; the proposer shapes for J-072's three slots; a token-identity checker; and a claim verdict that **mechanically refuses an identity claim grounded on a broken account and any speed claim grounded on accounting**. **13/13 test groups PASS** (§5), with the J-069 neighbour suite rebuilt at **11/11 PASS** and the pre-existing acceptance-arithmetic suite at **2/2 PASS**. One existing file was modified: a comment-only host contract in `aesir_engine/aesir.mojo` (+22 lines, no code).
- **No live claim is made anywhere in this doc.** The invariant is proven *by construction* (§3) for any round sequence whose accounts are complete and consistent — that is the claim this slice's roadmap text asks for ("the machinery can never change the text"). Live token-identity over real generations is **J-073's** proof and is NOT PROVEN here; any speed claim is NOT PROVEN here and is refused by the account until a live measurement grounds it (§7).

**The design (pre-registered).** Speculation in Aesir is a **draft-check pass in Part III's taxonomy (J-035)**: role = verify a proposed token sequence against the target model in one batch; design band width **2..16**. One round of the loop J-072 will build, in this account's terms:

- A **proposer** offers k draft tokens (k ≥ 1) for the positions after the committed prefix. Proposers never emit text.
- The **verify pass** forwards k+1 inputs in one batch — the pending emitted token plus the k drafts, under the tree's feed-the-emitted-token convention (the MoE `step()` forwards a token at `self.position`, then `position += 1`) — and the **target model** produces its own greedy choice at each of the k+1 positions: `target_greedy[0..k]`. The pass width is therefore k+1, so the band binds k to **1..15**.
- **Acceptance (greedy form):** the longest prefix *a* such that `draft[i] == target_greedy[i]` for every i < a. The round **emits a+1 tokens, every one of them taken from the target's list, never from the draft**: `emitted[i] = target_greedy[i]` for i ≤ a. Where the prefix runs the full k, `target_greedy[k]` is the **bonus** token; where it stops early, `target_greedy[a]` is the **correction** token. Draft tokens past the first mismatch are never emitted, even where a later draft coincidentally equals a later target choice — **acceptance is a prefix, not a filter**.
- **The invariant, by construction:** a plain greedy round emits exactly `target_greedy[0]` and advances one position. A speculative round emits `target_greedy[0..a]` and advances a+1 positions, landing in exactly the state the plain loop reaches after a+1 of its own rounds over the same choices (the verify pass's later choices are computed on contexts the plain loop would also build, because each accepted draft equals the target choice that precedes it). By induction over rounds, speculative greedy output is **token-identical to non-speculative greedy output** — not because drafts are usually right, but because the emitted stream is built from the target's choices only. A bad proposer costs speed, never text. This is the **greedy form** of the invariant; the sampled form (temperature / top-k / top-p with the rejection correction) is **J-082's** account, built on `core/speculative.mojo`'s existing min(1, p/q) arithmetic, and is **not** claimed here.
- **Rollback, exact:** the pass tentatively writes k+1 KV rows; the round commits `kv_before + a + 1` and discards (k+1) − (a+1) = **k − a rows**. In this tree's KVCache (fixed-capacity contiguous positions, append by explicit position, reads bounded by active length) discarding is a **length restore, not a byte clear**: rows past the restored length are never read and are overwritten by the next append at that position. Sampler state restores with it: the context-token count returns to `context_before + a + 1` and the **RNG state word is unchanged** — a greedy verify round consumes **zero RNG draws** (the sampler's greedy branch returns before any RNG use), and this account refuses a greedy round that reports a draw.

**Proposers (J-072's interface contract).** The census (J-006) documents four draft sources; the J-014 gate adjustment gives J-072's loop **three proposer slots**, and this account reconciles the two — the head slot is filled by either of the two `--mtp`-file forms:

- **SLOT_HEAD** — an MTP prediction head of the target itself (`PROPOSER_MTP_HEAD`: shares the target's forward state and tokenizer by construction, carries no KV of its own), or an assistant drafter model of the target's family (`PROPOSER_ASSISTANT_MODEL`: reads the target's KV per the census; tokenizer match required and verified, J-074).
- **SLOT_CROSS_MODEL** — a separate small model drafting for a large one (`PROPOSER_CROSS_MODEL`: carries its own KV; tokenizer identity required and verified, J-074).
- **SLOT_CONVERSATION** — guesses copied from the conversation itself (`PROPOSER_CONVERSATION`: no model, no KV, tokenizer trivially the session's own).

A proposer is described to the loop only by its `ProposerShape` (kind, slot, tokenizer-match requirement, own-KV flag, k cap). The loop never asks a proposer for probabilities in the greedy form, and no proposer output reaches the emitted stream except through `plan_verify`'s target-built emission.

**Placement.** The verify pass *is* a draft-check pass, so it is placed by Part III's engine exactly as one: `--drafts-on` (J-036, stored today behind `require_draft_check_use()`'s fail-closed gate, which names this slice and J-072), chosen by `core/draft_placement.mojo` (J-042) once a real executor exists for J-038/J-039 to measure. **This account changes no gate:** J-072 replaces the refusal with real consumption when it builds the loop, and only then.

**The verdict rule (pre-registered, implemented as `evaluate_spec_claim`).**

- **PERMITTED by account (construction alone):** the greedy identity claim for any round sequence whose every round is a complete, consistent `plan_verify` account — the emission is target-built by construction, so the identity needs no measurement luck.
- **REQUIRES LIVE:** a live token-identity claim (speculative vs plain greedy ids from a real model run) until the ids are filed and compared by this account's checker (J-073 owns the arch-matrix proof; the J-086 gate re-runs it); any speed / tok/s / acceptance-rate claim, always — accounting contains no speed and no acceptance statistic.
- **REFUSED outright:** an identity claim grounded on an incomplete or inconsistent account (a round whose target list is not exactly k+1, a width outside the draft-check band, a pass that cannot fit the KV capacity, a greedy round that consumed an RNG draw); a live identity claim whose filed ids diverge.

## 2. Inspection — what Aesir holds today (PROVEN by inspection)

Its draft bound is default 4, hard limit 64 (a different, older bound than Part III's design band; §3's account binds the verify pass to the J-035 band 2..16, and the two are not conflated anywhere in this slice).
- **`evaluate_acceptance` already returns the right shape** — prefix + rollback marker + KV step — but the engine facade's `speculative_generate` raises not-implemented, and `SpeculativeEngine` is referenced only by its own module and tests: **wired to no session**. There is no draft loop, no proposer, and no verify pass anywhere in the tree tonight. This slice builds the account the loop will consume; it does not pretend the loop exists.
- **Part III's gate names this slice.** `require_draft_check_use()` raises naming gate G1 and J-071/J-072; the placement harness classifies draft-check **UNMEASURABLE-WITH-REASON** (G1, no proxy); the draft-placement chooser (J-042) exists as Part V's input surface. The design's placement (§1) therefore lands on machinery that already refuses honestly rather than on a surface that silently no-ops.
- **The sampler makes the zero-draw invariant true.** In `sample_token_from_logits`, the greedy branch (temperature ≤ 0 or top_k == 1) precedes any RNG use, and the greedy tie-break is documented: equal finite maxima choose the lowest ID. The target's greedy choice at a position is therefore a deterministic function of the pass's logits — the premise the verify account's target list rests on.
- **RNG state is one word.** `RuneRNG`'s state is a single UInt64 word, so the host contract's snapshot/restore of sampler state is a one-word comparison (§3), not a state reconstruction.
- **The KV cache makes rollback a length restore.** `KVCache` is fixed-capacity contiguous positions [0, max_seq_len), no wrap/reorder; it appends by explicit position (`append` / `append_block`); the host forward bounds KV reads by `active_seq_len = min(start_pos + 1, max_seq_len)`. **No rollback/truncate machinery exists anywhere in `mimir_well.mojo`** — none is needed: rows past a restored length are dead by the read bound and overwritten by the next append at that position. J-072's rollback is therefore a length + counter restore (§3's host contract), not new cache machinery.
- **The session shape fits the pass.** The MoE session carries `kv_cache: KVCache` and a single position counter; `step()` forwards at `self.position` then `position += 1` — the feed-the-emitted-token convention §1's pass width (k+1 inputs: the pending token plus k drafts) is stated in. `SessionContext` carries `active_tokens` + `session_kv_offset`, the host session state the account's context count mirrors.

## 3. Design — the account

**`aesir_engine/core/speculative_verify.mojo`** (new, pure arithmetic in the J-066..J-069 pattern: it executes no forward, times nothing, opens no device, and contains no speed):

- **`plan_verify(...)`** — derives one greedy verify round from the proposer's draft list and the target's own greedy list: accepted prefix count, first-mismatch index (−1 when the prefix runs whole), the emitted list **built from the target list only** (accepted prefix + exactly one further target choice: the correction at the mismatch, or the bonus when the prefix ran whole), `kv_after = kv_before + accepted + 1`, `kv_rows_to_roll_back = k − accepted`, `context_after = context_before + accepted + 1`, and the round's RNG-draw count. **Refusals:** unknown proposer kind; k outside 1..15 (the pass width k+1 must sit in the draft-check design band 2..16); k above the proposer's cap; a target list not exactly k+1 long; any negative token id; a negative committed length or context; **a pass that cannot fit the KV capacity** (`kv_before + k + 1 > kv_capacity` — the host shortens k, J-079's business, rather than this account silently truncating); **any RNG draw reported for a greedy round** (greedy consumes none by construction — a draw means the round was not the greedy round claimed).
- **`plan_proposer(...)`** — the design's stated shape for each documented proposer (§1): model proposers capped at the band's k = 15; the conversation proposer capped at **8** — its guesses are copied text with the census's lowest documented hit character (~1/4), and a deeper conversation draft only widens the verify pass it usually wastes. Unknown kinds are refused, never defaulted.
- **`account_is_consistent(...)`** — the mechanical completeness check for a filed round account: width in the band and equal to k+1, accepted a prefix count within the draft, emitted exactly accepted+1 tokens, KV and context advancing by exactly the emitted count, rollback exactly the uncommitted remainder, no RNG draw, no negative emitted id. An account that fails this check grounds no identity claim (§1's rule) — it is restated, or the run is.
- **`identity_first_divergence(...)` / `sequences_identical(...)`** — the mechanical checker for the identity invariant: −1 / true means identical (same length, every token equal); a length difference with an equal shared prefix diverges at the shorter length; empty vs empty is identical. This is the checker J-073's live proof files its ids through.
- **`evaluate_spec_claim(...)`** — §1's verdict rule on every branch (construction identity / live identity / speed × permitted / requires-live / refused), in the J-067/J-069 claim-verdict vocabulary, with `proposer_kind_name` / `proposer_slot_name` / `claim_name` exact-name helpers.

**Host contract** (in the module header, binding on J-072's loop): (1) snapshot the session's committed KV length, context-token count, and RNG state word before proposing; (2) obtain k drafts from one proposer slot (1 ≤ k ≤ 15, and k ≤ the proposer's cap); (3) run **one** draft-check pass of width k+1 and record the target's greedy choice at every position — the target choices come from the pass, never from the proposer; (4) derive the round through `plan_verify` and emit exactly `account.emitted_tokens`; (5) restore KV length and context count to the account's restore points (rows past the length are dead by the read bound) and assert the RNG word is the snapshot's; (6) file the round's account with the run's evidence, so J-073's identity proof and J-075's acceptance instrumentation read accounts, not reconstructions.

**Registration in `tests/run_all.mojo` is not done**, for J-031 §10's stated reason (the branch line's counted-run pin cannot be re-accounted without the counted run, which belongs to an on-box leg) — the same standing practice as J-066..J-069.
