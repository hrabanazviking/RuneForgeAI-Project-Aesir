def merge_sorted(left, right):
    merged = []
    i = 0
    j = 0
    while i < len(left) and j < len(right):
        if left[i] <= right[j]:
            merged.append(left[i])
            i += 1
        else:
            merged.append(right[j])
            j += 1
    merged.extend(left[i:])
    merged.extend(right[j:])
    return merged

Tides are caused mainly by the gravity of the Moon. As the Moon orbits the Earth, its gravity pulls the ocean water toward it, making a bulge on the side facing the Moon and another bulge on the opposite side. As the Earth rotates, coastal places pass through these bulges and experience high tide, and between them they experience low tide. The Sun also plays a part, which is why spring tides happen when the Sun, Moon, and Earth line up.

def count_vowels(text):
    vowels = "aeiou"
    count = 0
    for ch in text.lower():
        if ch in vowels:
            count += 1
    return count
