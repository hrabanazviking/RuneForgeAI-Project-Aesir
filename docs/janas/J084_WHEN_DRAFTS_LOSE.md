### 1.1 The floor's arithmetic (exact, over the road's own currencies)

One speculative round emits `a + 1` tokens for its price (J-071: the accepted prefix plus exactly one target token), so a round's **gain over plain decode is exactly its accepted count `a`**: plain decode emits 1 token per target pass; the round emits `a + 1` tokens for one verify pass plus `k` draft steps. Over any set of rounds, with every cost stated by the host in **one** currency:

- `emitted = accepted + rounds`
- `value = emitted × plain_cost_per_token`
- `net = value − Σ round_cost` — **a LOSS iff `net < 0`** (break-even, `net == 0`, is not a loss).

Break-even mean acceptance per round is `a* = mean(round_cost) / plain_cost_per_token − 1`. Where a round's cost takes the **step form** — one verify pass priced as one plain step (J-071's one-pass accounting; the verify pass *is* a Part III draft-check pass) plus `k` draft steps at `r` plain steps each — the arithmetic closes: `a* = k·r`, and **the break-even acceptance rate is exactly `r`, at every k**. The floor is therefore a cost ratio, not an acceptance superstition: a pair pays on steps iff its acceptance rate beats its drafter's relative step cost.

When shallowing has done its work and the row still loses, the remaining honest move is to **stop** — which is the roadmap's sentence for this slice.

### 1.3 The disable rule (pre-registered in the header of `core/draft_loss_guard.mojo`, written before the code; the module's tests grade exactly this)

- **Identity:** one guard watches one (proposer kind, pair id, text type, language) row — J-075's row key, validated by J-075's own rules. Rows are never merged: the Italian-prose lesson is that a collapse hides inside aggregates, so Italian prose can disable while English prose keeps drafting.
- **Evidence:** J-071 `VerifyAccount`s only, gated on `account_is_consistent()` and kind match, each paired with the host-stated cost of that round in the guard's one currency. A refusal consumes nothing.
- **Window:** completed windows of 4 rounds — J-079's evidence quantum, imported (`LOSS_WINDOW_ROUNDS = ADAPT_WINDOW_ROUNDS`), never restated, so the two controllers decide over the same rounds: J-079 shallows first; this guard judges what remains. A partial window is PENDING and decides nothing.
- **Loss:** a window is LOSING iff its `net < 0` (strict). Break-even (`net == 0`) is counted and reported, never losing, and any non-losing window resets the consecutive-losing count.
- **Disable:** at the close of the **second consecutive losing window** — round 8 at the earliest — for the session. One losing window never disables (hysteresis: a single bad stretch is J-079's shallowing case, not a verdict).
- **Posture after disable (answered-locally, §7):** disabled **for the session**, no automatic re-probe — a probe round is itself a speculative round, so re-probing a proven-losing row burns the exact cost the guard exists to stop, and no filed evidence prices a safe probe cadence. Re-enabling is a host act (new session, or a new J-083 configuration). Recording into a disabled guard is refused by name, loudly.
- **Composition:** disabling is a runtime session state, not a configuration change — J-083's `draft_config.mojo` is **not edited** by this slice (the inspection recount asserts the tree diff is exactly this slice's new files); a disabled row's stored configuration validates and persists exactly as before. J-079's controller is likewise untouched.
- **Logging contract:** at disabling, `disable_record_text()` renders the record in a fixed, test-pinned field order (event, reason, row key, posture, plain cost, window + total accounts). The host files it beside the run's J-036 / J-083 records, in the same evidence set as the rounds' accounts — the J-036/J-044 precedent: the contract is stated and the record form is proven, and no session wiring is faked, because no session exists to wire into.

## 3. The guard's behaviour, as graded (SIMULATED fixtures, stated in the suite)

Fixtures are stated shapes traceable to filed numbers (§5): step-fixture round costs are the §1.1 step form at plain = 100 — 300 per k = 4 round at the worst-case admissible drafter (verify 100 + 4 × 50), 160 at the first pair's ratio (100 + 4 × 15); the byte fixture prices rounds from J-080's filed cold tax at k = 2 (a speculative token at 14710 units where a plain token costs 10000).

- **The losing stream disables at exactly round 8.** The Italian shape ([0,1,1,1] then [1,1,2,2] accepted at k = 4, the head of J-075's filed sequence) at worst-case costs: window 1 nets −500 (7 emitted × 100 − 1200, rate 1875 bp) and HOLDs; window 2 nets −200 (10 × 100 − 1200, rate 3750 bp) and DISABLES — totals at the freeze: 8 rounds, 32 drafted, 9 accepted, 17 emitted, cost 2400, value 1700, **net −700**; the disable record is pinned byte-for-byte in the suite.
- **The winning stream never disables.** The English shape (J-075's 34/40, six fully-accepted rounds) at the same worst-case costs nets +1400 over 10 rounds with zero losing windows.
- **The boundary stream behaves exactly as the rule states.** Accepted 2 of 4 every round (5000 bp — exactly the worst-case floor) at cost 300 nets **exactly 0** per window: three windows, no losing window, no disable — break-even holds forever, as §1.3 pre-registers.
- **Hysteresis is exact.** Losing, winning, losing windows leave the streak at 1 and nothing disabled after 12 rounds; the next losing window disables at round 16, not before.
- **The guard follows costs, not acceptance alone.** The *same* Italian stream at the first pair's step costs (160/round) closes both windows winning (net +600 over 10 rounds) and never disables — the suite pins §1.2's honest reading in code.
- **The byte currency convicts cold too.** Cold-cycle rounds at k = 2 priced per emitted token from J-080's +47.10 % tax disable at round 8 (totals: 16 drafted, 8 accepted, 16 emitted, cost 235360, net −75360).
- **Rows are separate.** Three guards fed in lockstep — Italian prose disables; English prose and code, same pair, same costs, keep drafting.
