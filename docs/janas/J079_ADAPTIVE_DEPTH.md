## 1. Verdict — and the tuning rule, pre-registered before measurement

**J-079 closes CLOSED-WITH-DEFERRED-LEG:** the adaptive-depth controller is built and proven — a pure, windowed tuning rule over J-071's verify accounts (and, read off filed evidence, over J-075's ledger rows through J-075's own rate), with its properties unit-tested and its behaviour against fixed k measured over stated fixtures at the SIMULATED class (§4). Tuning on a live pair is **DEFERRED-WITH-REASON** (§6): no proposer implementation, verify-pass executor, or session wiring exists in this tree, and no live pair is reachable tonight — so no live acceptance or speed figure is claimed, and none is faked.

- **The rule (pre-registered; the module header is its normative text):**
  1. **Evidence** — the controller consumes J-071 `VerifyAccount`s only, gated on `account_is_consistent()` and on the account's proposer kind matching the controller's kind (a refusal consumes nothing and leaves the controller exactly as it was). It recomputes nothing J-071 derived: a round's evidence is `(account.k drafted, account.accepted_count accepted)`. The same rule read off filed evidence is `decide_from_ledger_row()`, which takes an `AcceptanceRow` and reads its rate through J-075's own `rate_basis_points()` — never a restated rate.
  2. **Window / minimum evidence** — decisions are made only over a completed window of **ADAPT_WINDOW_ROUNDS = 4** consecutive rounds. Fewer rounds are **PENDING**: k does not move, however hot or cold the partial window looks. The window counters reset at every decision, so a decision always rests on exactly the last 4 rounds, never on a lifetime average. (For a ledger row, the deepen allowance scales with the row's evidence length in exact integer arithmetic — `wasted * 4 <= 2 * rounds` — so a long filed row is judged by the same proportion as a live window; a row under 4 rounds is PENDING.)
  3. **Decision (hysteresis deadband)** — over a window, with `wasted = drafted − accepted` and `rate_bp = accepted * 10000 // drafted`: **DEEPEN** only on near-saturation, `wasted <= ADAPT_DEEPEN_MAX_WASTED (2)` over the whole window — the drafts actually proposed were almost all accepted, so a deeper draft has evidence it would be used. **SHALLOW** when `rate_bp <= ADAPT_COLD_BP (3000)`, inclusive. Everything else is the deadband and **HOLDS**: a window can be warm (even 8500 bp) and still hold, when its wasted count says the extra depth was not being used.
  4. **Step** — a deepening window deepens k by **ADAPT_STEP = 2**; a cold window shallows k by 2. No other move exists.
  5. **Bounds** — k never leaves `[LOOP_K_MIN, the proposer's cap]`, the floor read from J-072 and the cap read from J-071's `plan_proposer()` on every decision, never restated here. Deepening at the cap and shallowing at the floor are HOLDs (counted as holds, never as moves); a step that would overshoot clamps exactly to the bound.
  6. **Start** — a session starts at J-072's `LOOP_K_INITIAL` (4) unless the host states another in-bounds start.
  7. **No-pathological-oscillation property (stated, tested)** — k changes at most once per window (at most one move per 4 rounds) and by at most ADAPT_STEP; any window whose rounds alternate hot and cold per-round (the fastest possible alternation) sits inside the deadband, so per-round alternation cannot move k at all; a reversal needs a full opposite window of evidence after the move.
- **Relation to J-072:** `suggest_next_k()` in the loop module is the unmeasured single-round SHAPE (deepen one on a fully accepted round, halve on a zero round, instantly). This module **supersedes** that shape as the tuning rule a live host uses: windowed, thresholded, and measured in this slice against fixed k (§4, SIMULATED fixtures). The loop function is not edited; `plan_adaptive_round()` plans each round's k through J-072's `plan_round_k()`, so the KV-capacity shortening stays J-072's recorded plan fact and is never this controller's silent move.

## 3. The rule's behaviour, in one paragraph

From the start k=4, a controller fed hot rounds climbs 4 → 6 → 8 → 10 … by one step per saturated window until the proposer's cap, where further saturated windows are holds; fed cold rounds it falls by one step per cold window to the floor k=1, where further cold windows are holds; fed anything in between — warm-but-wasteful windows, mid-rate windows, or per-round hot/cold alternation — it does not move at all. Every one of those sentences is a unit-tested property (§5), and §4 measures what the moves cost and save against never moving.

## 4. The tuning measured against fixed k (SIMULATED fixture replay)

`j079_measurement.py` replays deterministic acceptance fixtures through the §1 rule and through fixed-k baselines (k = 2, 4, 8) under a stated cost model: each regime is a repeating cycle of would-be prefix lengths L (the number of draft tokens the proposer would have matched at unbounded depth that round); a round at depth k drafts k, accepts `min(k, L)`, wastes `k − accepted`, and emits `accepted + 1` (J-071: the accepted prefix plus exactly one target token). The same L stream feeds every strategy, so every number below is a property of the stated fixtures, **SIMULATED**, never of a model pair. The L cycles are shaped from J-075's filed fixture shapes (English prose 34/40 kept at k=4, Italian prose 12/40, code 36/40, structured 31/40), extended past k=4 by the stated L values so deeper strategies have something to accept; the extension is part of the fixture statement, not a measurement of a model. 48 rounds per regime. - No Janas material was consulted; every design question was answered from Aesir's own architecture, and is recorded here as answered-locally: (a) the **window length 4, step 2, cold threshold 3000 bp, and deepen allowance 2 wasted** are Aesir's own stated constants — the roadmap names the behaviour ("deepen when hot, shallow when cold"), not constants; 4 rounds is the shortest window in which J-075's filed fixture shapes separate cleanly, and the step of 2 keeps a full climb from the start to the model cap inside three windows; (b) **deepen keys on wasted count, not on rate** — see the rejected variant below; (c) the **ledger-row deepen allowance scales with evidence length** in exact integers, so a filed row and a live window are judged by one proportion, never by two rules; (d) the controller **supersedes J-072's `suggest_next_k` shape for live hosts** rather than editing it — the shape stays in the loop module as the filed J-072 record, and this slice's header states the supersession where a host will read it; (e) **capacity shortening is never the controller's move** — it stays J-072's plan fact, recorded on the plan, with the controller's own k untouched by planning.
