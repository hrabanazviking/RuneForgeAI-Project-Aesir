## 5. The verdict rule (pre-registered)

Stated in `core/prefill_grade.mojo`'s header and here **before** the §6 filed grades were run through it. Per step (rank *r*, gap *g* in milli-logits): **(S1)** EXACT iff *r* == 0. **(S2)** NEAR-TIE iff *r* ≤ 2 **and** *g* < 1000 (sub-logit) — both conjuncts bind. **(S3)** DIVERGENT otherwise. Per cell: **(C1)** NOT_GRADED iff no step records exist. **(C2)** COMPARATOR_ARTIFACT iff the comparator check failed — precedence over every step pattern; check the comparator before suspecting the engine. **(C3)** STEP0_BOUNDARY iff comparator-valid, divergence is confined to step 0, and every later step is exact or near-tie — its own class: neither a pass nor an engine divergence; its disposition is §7's boundary re-grade. **(C4)** FAITHFUL iff every step is exact. **(C5)** NEAR_TIE iff no step diverges and at least one is near-tie — a **pass** verdict under the road's standing discipline (near-tie band = faithful), kept distinct from FAITHFUL so a near-tie cell is never filed as perfect. **(C6)** DIVERGENT otherwise, with `first_divergent_step` naming where the evidence starts.

One honesty clause rides with the rule, as in J-057–J-059: **it grades records, never the runs that produced them.** A filed-evidence cell (§6) inherits its run's provenance and honesty class; the account's agreement with a filed grade proves the account reproduces the road's reading of that grade — it does not re-run, re-measure, or upgrade the grade.

## 8. Findings and defects

- **No correctness defect found** in the code or grades inspected: both filed grade texts re-verify through the account to exactly the verdicts J-022's prose recorded (post-fix faithful-under-near-tie; pre-fix divergent from step 5), J-034's re-parse constants agree with the account's counts, and the sessions/forms inspected in J-053–J-059 stand as their slices recorded. The §3 B-items are **absences** (the build list), recorded with owners — B-DRIVER and B-ORACLE with §7's leg, the M/G items with their owning slices.
- **The B1 lesson, restated as battery doctrine (the reason this battery exists):** J-022's pre-fix grade caught a real defect — the streamed session **renormalised its top-8 router weights** where the comparator's OLMoE graph weights experts by **raw softmax probabilities** — that id-level fluency had hidden (the pre-fix output was coherent for five tokens, then word salad; ranks said so at step 5). Removing the renorm produced the post-fix NEAR_TIE grade. Two standing consequences for every future cell: (i) a form is not faithful because its output reads well — only the grade says so; (ii) the comparator-artifact check comes first precisely because the same grade shape (exact while young, compounding later) is also what a boundary artifact would mimic at step 0 — §5's C2/C3 ordering is B1's lesson encoded.
- **A reading correction recorded for citers:** J-022 §3 counts "rank ≤ 2" steps (10/24 pre-fix) as the near-tie band in prose; the roadmap's rule is conjunctive (rank ≤ 2 **with** sub-logit gap), and under it the pre-fix grade has exactly **one** near-tie step. Battery filings use the conjunctive rule only (§5); the prose count must not be cited as a battery number.
