## 4. The placement design, as built (Part III's as-built summary)

- **The harness (J-038).** Bounded micro-runs per pass kind per candidate under a settle + rounds discipline (settle discarded, median of rounds, per-candidate and per-sweep hard bounds, bound-stopped never ranked), with candidate honesty inherited by everything above it: every candidate is measured, EXCLUDED, or UNMEASURABLE-WITH-REASON — never silently dropped. The choice is kept for the session and reported with its reasons. Outcomes as filed: prompt DEFAULT-CONFIRMED, reply DEFAULT-BY-NOISE, draft-check NO-MEASURED-FALLBACK (every candidate unmeasurable-with-reason through the harness's own classification — the pass's absence is checked before inventory truth, and no synthetic proxy was ever measured in its name).
